# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-08
- Three sit-together games on one word list: the moving garden ("فين …؟"), balloons (blow or hold, pop, "كمان؟"), and complete-the-picture.
- 41 everyday Egyptian Arabic picture words in 6 groups; 10 in practice at a time, picked by weight (words he understands but does not say come most; mastered words come back now and then for review).
- Parent review behind a 2-second hold: mark 👆 🔁 ⭐ ➕ ✋ per day; a word said alone on 3 days within a week can be confirmed as mastered, and a new word comes in from the group with the fewest words; swap any word; stuck words are marked after two weeks.
- The parent records every line in their own voice; clips stay on the device. Music goes quiet under every line.
- Works offline and from the Home Screen. Nothing about the child leaves the device.
