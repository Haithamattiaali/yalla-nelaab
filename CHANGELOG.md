# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-11-08
- Focus: the child's screen has nothing to press but the game. Next, home, music and the review moved into a parent bar: hold the faint corner 2 seconds, then confirm at the other corner. The parent moves on with a 1-second press anywhere once the word moment is over.
- Touch: a tap counts for the nearest thing (no need to be precise); things move at half speed; balloons fill with taps (about 8); no pinch zoom, page scroll or long-press menus.
- What to do now: a hand shows him: on the balloon until his first tap, on the right thing after 5 quiet seconds or a wrong tap (it glows), on a puzzle part after a few quiet seconds. A new word shows its picture in the bubble until he shows he knows it.
- Sound: louder through a compressor; the parent's recordings are trimmed and brought to full loudness; on iPhone/iPad the sound stays on the loud speaker (and plays with the silent switch on) and goes back to it after the microphone.
- Calmer scene: no drifting cloud or big tree behind the game; a bigger balloon.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-08
- Three sit-together games on one word list: the moving garden ("فين …؟"), balloons (blow or hold, pop, "كمان؟"), and complete-the-picture.
- 41 everyday Egyptian Arabic picture words in 6 groups; 10 in practice at a time, picked by weight (words he understands but does not say come most; mastered words come back now and then for review).
- Parent review behind a 2-second hold: mark 👆 🔁 ⭐ ➕ ✋ per day; a word said alone on 3 days within a week can be confirmed as mastered, and a new word comes in from the group with the fewest words; swap any word; stuck words are marked after two weeks.
- The parent records every line in their own voice; clips stay on the device. Music goes quiet under every line.
- Works offline and from the Home Screen. Nothing about the child leaves the device.
